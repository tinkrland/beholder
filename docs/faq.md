# faq

**is this a marketing tool?**
no. no ad copy, posting, scheduling, seo, or brand work. beholder produces pitches and positioning for applications, demos, and conversations.

**do i need a repo?**
no. paste a description or upload docs. a repo is one input among several.

**what if i do not have a rubric?**
use a preset (yc, emergent ventures), or paste a brief and beholder drafts a custom lens you can edit.

**will it make up traction or metrics?**
never. missing evidence shows up as a gap, with what would close it.

**how current are the presets?**
every preset shows its sources and a last-verified date. stale presets show a warning.

**what if my rubric has vague criteria like "innovation"?**
you get a score range and the reasoning, plus the evidence that would move it.

**what if a sponsor track and the main track conflict?**
each is its own target, and beholder shows which fits best. the consistency check flags framings that would clash if one judge saw the other's version.

**can i paste my own pitch and have it checked?**
yes. beholder checks each claim against your facts and rewrites the ones that overreach.

**does it guarantee i will win?**
no. it tells you how a target will likely read your project and what is missing. it cannot predict decisions.

**is my data used to train models?**
no. see [privacy.md](privacy.md).

**why not just ask a general chatbot to rewrite my pitch for investors?**
a general chatbot has no target-specific lens, no evidence binding, and no memory of what contradicts what across your other pitches. beholder scores against the real rubric and flags what the evidence does not support.

**who decides whether a claim is supported?**
you do. beholder proposes; you confirm, reject, or edit facts. confirmed user-stated facts are labeled as such in the output.
