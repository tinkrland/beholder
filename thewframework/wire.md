# wire

dimension: messaging, social layer and virality. product management
function: growth engineering and community loops.

this is the dimension where beholder holds its sharpest line. it reads the
wire; it does not spin it.

## what beholder extracts

- **messaging facts**: stated positioning, one-liners, and language from
  growth docs, landing copy, and readme taglines. kept as statements your
  sources make, with citations.
- **community and loop statements**: what sources say about referral,
  sharing, community, or distribution loops. a stated loop is a fact; a
  hoped-for one is not.
- **audience resonance facts**: which vocabulary your own audience-facing
  docs already use, because consistency with your own voice is part of
  positioning.

## how beholder addresses it

- the lens's `vocabulary` field steers generated sentences toward the reader's
  language; that is positioning, and it is as far as beholder goes.
- the one-liner and category frame sections are the messaging surface of the
  view: the same story, tuned per eye.
- a wire claim ("viral by design") passes through the same overclaim finder as
  everything else. growth language overreaches more than any other kind, and
  beholder treats it accordingly.

## what beholder never does

- no ad copy, no social posts, no scheduling, no seo, no brand identity, no
  campaign analytics. output is for an application, a demo, a partner
  conversation, or a pitch.
- it never manufactures virality language. if no source states a loop, the
  wire dimension is a gap, and beholder says so rather than inventing one.
