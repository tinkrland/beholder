# why not

dimension: barriers, friction and objections. product management function:
risk mitigation and onboarding design.

this is the dimension beholder is built around. most positioning tools
flatten it; beholder treats it as first-class.

## what beholder extracts

- **objection seeds**: from the lens, not the project. every lens carries
  `objection_seeds` and `red_flags`: what this reader doubts, fears, and
  rejects.
- **alternative facts**: stated alternatives and competitors, the "why not
  just use x" candidates, with citations.
- **friction facts**: stated onboarding requirements, setup steps, and
  technical prerequisites, read from docs and code shape. what it takes to
  start is a fact, not a footnote.

## how beholder addresses it

- "objections and honest answers" is a fixed view section, and the answer to
  each objection rests on facts or says plainly what is not there.
- the gap finder is the why-not engine: low-support criteria, ranked by
  weight, each with what would close them.
- the overclaim finder turns friction into honesty: claims that outrun the
  evidence get defensible rewrites, and verdicts land as supported, partial,
  unsupported, or contradicted.
- the honesty guard is the enforcement: a sentence resting on nothing fails
  the render, and export stays blocked until facts are confirmed or sentences
  are dropped.

## what beholder never does

- it never hides an objection because it is inconvenient. what not to lead
  with exists precisely so the hard parts have a place.
- it never manufactures reassurance. the system can only point to a gap; it
  never creates evidence to close one.
